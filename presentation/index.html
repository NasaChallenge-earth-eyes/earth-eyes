6 consecutive years of drought have broken historical rainfall patterns and depleted groundwater.

The Wheat Monoculture Trap
  • • Smallholders continue planting rainfed wheat out of tradition.
  • • Late-spring heatwaves (Chergui) decimate wheat flowering and grain filling.
  • • Harvest failures reach up to 60%, pushing family farms into bankruptcy.

🧠 The Resilient Rotation Algorithm

A transparent, multi-criteria agronomic decision engine ranking alternatives against conventional wheat.

Decision Metrics
  • 1. Win Rate vs Wheat: Percentage of 41 historical seasons where the alternative crop beats wheat economically.
  • 2. Water Productivity (MAD / m³): Economic return generated per cubic meter of total water consumed.
  • 3. Nitrogen Fixation: Soil fertility restoration bonus (+35 kg N/ha for legumes).
Sanitary & Weed Disruption
  • • Breaks chronic cereal disease cycles (Fusarium, Septoria).
  • • Seamlessly connects with TerroirAI's leaf disease diagnostic CNN.
  • • Maximizes resilience against severe rainfall deficits.
